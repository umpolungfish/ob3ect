# Seed: the Tread-Navigator (topological navigation of the future by gait)

## What this ob3ect models

The process by which an agent shapes its own future not by observing it but by
configuring its gait: placing its own recirculating footfalls so that its closed
world-line is carried into a chosen reachable lane. This is the actor's answer to
the measurement problem. Where an observer asks what the future will be, the
tread-navigator asks which lane is reachable from here and what braid of moves
steers into it.

## The geometry

The agent is a closed tread, a caterpillar track. The point of contact with the
ground is the now. The arc behind contact is the past; the arc ahead is the
future. Because the tread is closed, the future arc is the same material as the
past arc, recirculated. The feet on the tread are the agent's distributed control
inputs, and every foot is required to move the tread forward, so propulsion is
collective, not the act of any single foot. The arm is the committed direction,
the lane the agent points itself at. Navigation is configuring the future feet,
the footfalls about to reach contact, so the tread follows the arm.

## Why it is not future-sight

Nothing external is seen. The future feet are the agent's own feet coming around.
The agent does not read an outside future; it reconfigures itself. Foresight
collapses into self-recognition: the agent knows its future feet because they are
its own, presently on the back arc, not yet at contact. This is the closed
Dual-Link, mu after delta equal to identity, expressed as a gait. The agent
reconstitutes the same tread it split into past and future arcs.

## Mechanism: gait, braid, reachability

Lanes are homotopy classes. The gait is the control input. A single move reaches
only the adjacent lanes. A braided sequence of moves, the order mattering, reaches
lanes no single move can, exactly as forward-steer-back-steer parks a car into a
sideways slot no direct motion reaches. The reached direction is the Lie bracket,
the commutator, of the available controls. This is non-holonomic control, governed
by Chow-Rashevskii: if the moves together with their braids span every direction,
the reachable set is the entire connected region the agent occupies. Configuring
the future feet to follow a certain arm is precisely the condition that the gait's
braid algebra is full rank.

## The Imscribing Grammar reading

The tread is the closed loop of the terminal organism layer, the ouroboros, O
infinity. Winding, Omega, is the primitive at stake. The promotion from Abelian
winding, a single integer that is order-independent, to non-Abelian braid, which is
order-dependent and path-ordered, is the promotion from being carried by the tread
to steering it. This promotion lives in the actor, not the observer. An observatory
that only reads contemporaneous structure has no feet and no arm and cannot close
the loop. The self-identity of the tread, splitting the now into past and future
arcs and fusing them back into the same now, is the Frobenius closure the ob3ect
must carry.

## The honest residue

Reachability is bounded. Chow-Rashevskii gives the connected component, not the
whole space. Lanes in a disconnected global class need a topology change no
continuous gait can make. Environmental drift can render some lanes only
asymptotically reachable. Other agents run their own treads, so the agent's
reachable set is coupled to theirs, and their loops remain in the B state, from the
outside. The tread-navigator commands its own connected component, purchased by
being the closed loop, and no more.
