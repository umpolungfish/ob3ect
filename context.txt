 The trunk is a lattice transformer trained autoregressively on certified turns of the form ⊢⟨word⟩◇⊞⟨⟩●⟨flow·sink·kernel⟩⊣, where the word rides the δ arm and the oracle-labeled readout rides the μ arm.Two populations exist to feed it. The real organisms: 879 protocol words drawn from the living catalog, kernel-labeled 830 T / 49 B / 0 F — closure is their norm. The synthetic population: 1000 generated words, labeled 517 T / 483 B — failure is half their diet. Three regimens have been run and measured at the mouth, oracle-judged. Real alone: fluency 7/24 kernel-green, self-verdicts spoken only as T — the trunk cannot pronounce its own failure. Mixed 1:1: B self-calls appear and are correct, fluency drops to 5/24, word-style degrades toward babble idiom. Real doubled against synthetic 2:1: both capacities degrade, 4/24, though single turns achieve full self-closure in both polarities — the capacity coexists in one vessel but has not stabilized. The regimen to be designed: how the two populations compose into the trunk's training — in sequence, in proportion, or by structural placement within the turn — such that both capacities hold at once.
