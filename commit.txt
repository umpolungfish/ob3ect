Demotion can reach every funded provider, and never borrows a foreign model.

Two faults, found when a design run died reporting that every funded
provider had failed while two funded accounts sat unused.

The demotion list named only two providers. Qwen and Google are both fully
implemented and both had keys present on this host, but neither could ever
be demoted to, so the chain ran out at the second entry and the run
aborted. Both are in the list now, and Google is keyed by the name the
provider defaults table actually uses: a miss there is silent and its
consequence is the second fault.

The default-model lookup fell back to a hardcoded slug from another vendor
entirely. A model slug is provider-scoped, so a missing entry turned into a
404 against a healthy endpoint and buried the real fault, which is that the
provider had no default configured. It raises now and says so.
