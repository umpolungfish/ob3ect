--churn winds the pipeline back over its own output.

Zoom moves across granularity: coarser and finer descriptions of one thing.
Churn moves inward. It imscribes the description, folds the resulting ob3ect
back into the context that produced it, and then imscribes each of that
ob3ect's own steps as a description in its own right.

The folding is what makes it churn rather than a batch. A child is imscribed
from its step held inside the ob3ect the step belongs to, held in turn inside
the original description. Without that the children are twelve unrelated
ob3ects that happen to share a parent.

Depth is counted in windings, one winding being a full turn of the pipeline
over its own output, which is the right unit here and not "levels": the second
turn is not a finer description of the first, it is the first gone round again
with itself in hand. One winding is the root alone. Two imscribes every step of
the root. Three winds each of those, so the count grows as the product of the
step counts and --max-ob3ects stops the walk rather than letting it run away.

The report's closing line is the one worth reading. A step that closes in place
but fails when imscribed alone is the interesting case: the parent has closed
over something that does not close by itself.

Verified against a stubbed designer: tree shape, winding depth, ob3ect count,
manifest, and that the child context carries the parent's word and the original
description.
