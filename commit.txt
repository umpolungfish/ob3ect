Make the print theme fit a page, not just lose its background

Clearing the background was necessary and not sufficient. On a page the side view
still carried a constants box in the frame corner and a dozen labels stacked in
one square inch of the mainline.

Print now omits the alpha and mass-ratio box: those are derived quantities that
belong to the caption or the text, and in the corner they are chrome.

Print also drops the labels that collide. The three A2 root marks project onto
the x-axis in this plane, so two of them land on the same point and their labels
sat on top of FFUSE3 and horn(0); they are a face-on fact and the equatorial
view is where they read. The syzygy and phi-tangent labels and three of the four
chord labels go the same way, leaving lambda_C, which is what the section is
about. The remaining labels are lifted off the marks they name.

And the limits are tightened. The screen view pads the section into a square that
is mostly empty, and at column width that empty field is what gets scaled, so the
geometry shrinks inside its own frame.
