revalidate_straus: re-check built ob3ects against the corrected gates

Artifacts designed while Axiom C was a biconditional and the Frobenius verdict
had to be PASS/FAIL carry a stale grounding_status and a stale is_valid_ob3ect,
while their tuples and phases are untouched. Re-running the checks is the repair:
nothing is regenerated and nothing is hand-edited. The slot order is taken from
the gate rather than retyped, so there is one copy of it.

The three built so far all come back grounding=full, valid=True, verdict=T — the
gates were the obstruction, not the objects.
