The Erdos objects nest, in the form the pipeline takes

I had written twelve prose files and called them prepared ob3ects. They are not:
a batch is a yaml carrying the design — domain, scope, provider, temperature,
retries, context path, and the catalog entries to inject — with the entities as
the imscription targets. That is what erdos_objects.yaml now is, and the prose
files are gone rather than left beside it.

The entities name the object wanted and no candidate: the least vertex count
forcing a monochromatic copy of one of two graphs, the lift carrying a hypergraph
Ramsey bound up a uniformity, the proportion of the plane covered by congruent
discs, a partition relation holding in one model and failing in another, the
least rung at which the ladder product carries a divisor at minus one.

Context entries are taken from the catalog by name — ramsey_numbers_general,
ramsey_3_hypergraph, sphere_packing_density_open, continuum_hypothesis,
erdos_straus_conjecture and the rest — since a tuple is sourced, never written.
