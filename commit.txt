ground_native: the tuple the native designer does not mint

ask --ob3ect computes the structural faces in Rust and writes a valid artifact,
but it never runs the imscription gate, so its JSON carries no 12-primitive tuple
and no grounding status while auto.py's does. Two shapes for one kind of thing is
the drift this corpus keeps finding.

This closes the gap from the side that is missing rather than by dropping the
field from the other, and it mints through the SAME guided gate auto.py calls —
_run_gated_imscription, imported, not copied. A tuple is sourced, never written.
