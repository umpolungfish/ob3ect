Let plotter3 select views, and size itself for a page

Two additions on top of the print theme, both forced by putting the figure on an
actual page rather than looking at it on screen.

--views keeps only the panels asked for. Three panels thirty inches wide are
illegible once scaled into a text column: the labels survive the vector export
and die on the page. All three are still drawn, because the draw code for each
is written against its own axes, and the unwanted ones are removed before the
layout is computed.

The panel is sized for 1:1 placement in print. bbox_inches crops to the drawn
content, which is roughly a third of the declared width because the axes carry
wide empty margins, so the saved file is much narrower than the panel and
including it at \linewidth MAGNIFIES it. A seven-inch panel cropped to 186pt and
blown up to a 390pt column took 8pt labels to about 17pt, which is how a figure
ends up with type larger than the body text it sits in. Fifteen inches lands the
crop near column width, and there the declared point sizes mean what they say.

Print also drops the panel title, which repeats the caption and is the one label
guaranteed to collide with the plot at column width.
