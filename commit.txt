A context repository for the Straus frontier, and six ob3ect asks

Seven files under context/straus_frontier carrying what an ob3ect run needs to
answer about this target without being told what to answer.

The target is one statement: EveryNClosed, which gives Erdős-Straus on the whole
surviving class by a proved reduction, so the conjecture and it stand or fall
together. The rest of the repository says what closure at a rung is, how the
class collapses onto a single numerator under the greedy step, which three layers
close it without search, that what they miss sits on n ≡ 1 (mod 24), and that on
the frontier the criterion becomes a question about a multiplicative span against
the exponents a square supplies.

The measurements are separated from the statements, and marked as measurements:
every value in the class below 200000 closes; the rung height is flat against the
fourth root at C ≈ 3.6 across two orders of magnitude, so the depth to control is
O(n^(1/4)) and a bounded-ceiling target is the wrong one; the gap between span
membership and exponent supply thins from 5.0% to 1.5% as more primes enter.

06_instruments records the route, because the route is the tools. Vox lifts a
proof term and its verdict IS the proof's state — B locates the arm left open
rather than reporting failure, which is the debug loop. The kernel walk computes a
claim instead of printing it, and three walks on this corpus failed before they
held with the mathematics right and the walk wrong each time. The designer
supplies the object where the development lacks the one a statement is about.

PROMPTS.md holds six asks, deliberately short: the rigid form carries the
structure and a long brief steers the answer conventional. Each describes the
object positively and names no candidate, so nothing is laundered back as a
finding. Two of the six may return one object under two readings, which is worth
knowing and is why they are not merged.

Prepared, not run.
