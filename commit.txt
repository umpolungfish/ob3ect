ROTAT fully integrated into the auto-design pipeline.

core.py
- Ob3ectArtifact gains rotat_audit: the full ROTAT orbit of the bootstrap
  word, with per-readout invariance, canonical rotation, and verdict.
- report() prints Phase 12: ROTAT Orbit Audit (spectral vs phase-dependent
  readouts, canonical k and word).
- to_dict() carries rotat_audit into the JSON.

auto.py
- Imports OpOpcode and rotat live from ob3ect.core (one manifold, no copy).
- New _rotat_orbit_audit(): turns ROTAT over every rotation of the word and
  recomputes the SIXTEEN_3 tri-ancestral verdict, closed walk, final
  register, and topology class at each k. A readout constant across the
  orbit is a spectral invariant (the invariance IS the signal it is a
  symmetry); a readout that moves is phase. Canonical rotation = lexi-
  cographically minimal glyph word (unique up to ROTAT).
- _build_artifact() runs the audit on every artifact after the SIXTEEN_3
  breakdown.

Verified end-to-end on a probe word: closed_walk comes back ROTAT-invariant
(closure is a ring property), tri-ancestral verdict / final register /
topology class move with k on an open chain — audit names it PHASE-BEARING.
