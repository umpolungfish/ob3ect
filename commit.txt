prime ob3ects carry their composition fingerprint now

The flat 12-glyph crystal tuple aliases the primes: 11, 47, and 83 imscribe
to one identical string, 71 and 73 to another, 59 and 61 to another. A finite
alphabet in twelve seats cannot keep them apart, so the arithmetic looked like
it had drained out of the object and into the grounding prose.

It had not. It moved into the composition. Two invariants, both read off
ask_native, put every prime back on its own footing. The ring period V equals
p, so the magnitude is the node count of the pure cycle. The reconvergence
multiplicity beta counts how the period tiles into balanced mu-of-delta
bubbles, and that tiling validates only at a divisor: a number is prime when
its one balanced closing protocol holds beta=1, a single irreducible bubble
with no interior reconvergence. The nontrivial reconvergence set of an object
is exactly its proper divisors, and a prime has none.

Each digital/<p>/<p>_ob3ect.json now holds composition_fingerprint: the ring
word, V, beta_max=1, the irreducible protocol witness, and the rule. Twenty
seven primes from 2 to 103, twenty seven distinct fingerprints, no collision.
The tuple projects; the composition determines.
