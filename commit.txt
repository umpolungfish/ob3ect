The loaders index by the new primitive keys

auto.py and editorial.py read IG_catalog.json by axis key, and the twelve axes
now carry the IMASM alphabet. ⊙ stays on Criticality, so entries whose
criticality value is the monad are untouched.

editorial.py's own PRIM_ORDER is in a different axis order from every other site
in the tree. That is a pre-existing disagreement and it is left as it was: the
rename moved its keys, not its order, so nothing that read it before reads
differently now.
