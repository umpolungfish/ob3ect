An empty answer switches provider, and parse retries are bounded

A provider that returns no text at all was being treated as a model that
answered badly: the parse raised, the parse branch counted it as the model's
mistake and asked again, and --retries defaults to infinity, so it asked forever.
Nothing in the output said what was wrong — just banners.

An empty response is now its own failure, named, printed, and answered by moving
to the next provider. And a parse failure gets three attempts at one provider
before the chain moves on, however large --retries is: the unbounded budget is
for rate limits and transient HTTP, where waiting is the right move, not for
asking a model the same question a thousand times.
