A grounding failure now says what failed.

Two ob3ects in a row came back with is_valid_ob3ect true, lean_verified true,
and grounding_status "failed", and nothing anywhere in either artifact said
which primitive had failed or why. The information was not missing. The gate
computes failed_primitives and auto.py read grounding_status and reasoning and
dropped it, so the verdict arrived without its cause and could not be acted on.

The artifact carries grounding_failed_primitives now, it is serialized, the
summary prints it, and the tuple context handed to the design call names the
ungrounded primitives rather than passing a failed grounding along silently as
though it were settled.

A status without its cause is not a verdict.
