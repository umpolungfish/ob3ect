The trilattice reports the word it walked, in the marks the Grammar uses

Three faults in one block of the Phase 11 breakdown, found in the artifact for
the cosmic-winding ob3ect, which grounded full and verified in Lean while its own
breakdown printed a word the artifact does not contain.

The GLYPH table still spelled AFWD and AREV as ASCII > and <. imasm_core emits
≻ and ≺, so the printed step table showed two marks that no longer exist while
the steps beside them were current.

json_report did not report the word it walked. It stripped every VINIT and TANCH
out of the interior and re-wrapped the remainder in ⊢…⊣ — a second construction
of an object that already exists. The ob3ect's word carries an interior ⊣, so it
came back thirteen glyphs as a twelve-glyph word, disagreeing with the table
printed directly beneath it and with nothing to say which one was the word. It
now joins the walked sequence and nothing else.

And the closing line collapsed two readings into one warning. A T verdict against
an open walk is not a failure to be warned about; it is the tri-ancestral reading
and the walk reading disagreeing, which is a fact about the object. This artifact
is exactly that case — verdict T, closed walk false — and it printed only
"not fully closed", throwing the informative half away. Four outcomes now, each
said.

Verified: the ob3ect's own thirteen-step sequence now reports
⊢⊣∈≻⊤≺⊥⋈⊙⊞∋◻⊣, identical to its glyph_word.

Noted, not fixed: Vox and ob3ect each carry an imasm16_3_core.py and they have
diverged in architecture, not only in content. In Vox the glyph IS the opcode
value, so there is no name-to-symbol boundary for a mismatch to hide behind. In
ob3ect the constants are names and GLYPH is a translation table — which is the
boundary that rule exists to abolish, and it is where all three of these hid.
