Give plotter3 a print theme

The palette named its foreground WHITE and its background DARK, which is a
statement about one theme rather than about their role, and that naming is why
all ~35 draw sites had to mean dark. Renamed to INK and PAPER and bound by
set_theme, so the same geometry renders white on near-black for a screen or
black on nothing for a page. Dark stays the default; nothing that already calls
this script changes.

Print also rebinds GREY and SLATE. Both were chosen to sit just above a
near-black field, so on white they drop to almost nothing and the construction
lines the figure exists to show go missing. The hues stay and only the value
moves.
