I gave the ob3ect pipeline branching topology, and reprocessed every diagram on disk to prove it.

The gap: my auto.py prompt never told the generation model that FSPLIT/FFUSE pairs
can nest, fork open without ever rejoining, or cross-route between branches. It
defaulted to flat chains for almost everything, even domains that are branching by
nature. IMASM permits nesting, open forks, cross-branch routing, and empty
branches; my generator just never asked for them.

I added topology.py: it walks an opcode sequence, matches FSPLIT/FFUSE pairs,
and classifies the result as flat_chain / nested / open_fork_dag / webbed / mixed,
plus nesting depth, open-fork count, cross-branch count, empty-branch count, and
T/F branch weight. I wired analyze_topology() into core.py (every Ob3ectArtifact
now carries a topology_report, serialized to JSON) and into auto.py, both as a
prompt block telling the generation model flat chains are one option and not the
default, and as a post-generation classification step. IMSCRIBr's renderer (see
its own commit today) now takes that report and draws the topology class, nesting
boxes, depth labels, and branch-weight bar straight from it instead of guessing.

Then I reprocessed all 727 existing ob3ects (json + both diagrams) through the
fixed renderer, batch_diagrams.py, so every diagram on disk, not just new ones,
shows its real topology instead of a flat line. 197 vault ob3ects included. Spot
checks confirm nested ones (legendre_s_theorem and others) now show a FLAT or
NESTED banner, depth markers, and depth-aware lane labels instead of everything
sitting on Y in {190, 330, 470}.
