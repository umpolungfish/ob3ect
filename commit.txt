A nest for what the corpus cannot yet say

Twelve imscriptions, eleven of them for objects a removed statement needed and
the development lacks: the Ramsey number of a pair, the size-Ramsey number,
hypergraph Ramsey with its stepping-up map, edge-disjoint cycles, the
girth-chromatic function, packing density, the self-avoiding walk exponent, the
Pasch count, a partition relation that forks at B under the ambient axioms, and
the list chromatic threshold. The twelfth is the rung bound.

Each describes the object wanted and names no candidate. RUN.md carries the
table of what each unblocks and the command, which is Lando's to run.
