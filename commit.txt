I seeded a new ob3ect for the tread-navigator (ctx/tread_navigator_seed.md).

It formalizes the model I reached working through future-sight: an agent shapes
its future not by observing it but by configuring its gait, placing its own
recirculating footfalls so its closed world-line is carried into a chosen
reachable lane. The tread is a closed loop, so the future feet are the past feet
recirculated, and foresight collapses into self-recognition (the closed Dual-Link,
mu after delta equals identity, expressed as a gait). The steering primitive is
the non-Abelian braid: braided moves reach lanes no single move can, the reached
direction being the Lie bracket of the controls, as in parallel parking.
Chow-Rashevskii sets the reachable set to the whole connected component when the
gait's braid algebra is full rank. Omega winding is the primitive at stake, and
the promotion from Abelian winding to non-Abelian braid lives in the actor, not
the observer.

The seed is prepared for the auto.py design pipeline to imscribe and
Frobenius-verify. I did not run it or hand-write the tuple.
