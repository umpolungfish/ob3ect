ROTAT: op-opcode added to the kernel

core.py gains OpOpcode (an enum of a different order than the twelve node
Opcodes) and rotat(ops, k), the cyclic shift of a word. The twelve are the
wheel; the op-opcode is the axis the wheel turns on, the thirteenth of the
crown's order rather than the rim's. ROTAT is the ring automorphism, the
Weyl-Heisenberg shift; rotat^len is the identity and the multiset is invariant.
