An object that measures, for a positive integer M and a modulus r coprime to M,
the difference between two sets: the multiplicative subgroup of the units modulo
r generated by the primes dividing M, and the image modulo r of the divisors of
M squared. The object must express when the two coincide, must express the
deficiency when they do not, and must take as its central quantity the exponent
available to each prime rather than the primes themselves.
