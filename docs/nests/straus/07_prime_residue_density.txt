An object that expresses, for odd n congruent to 1 modulo 24, the probability
that the product n(n+3)/4 has at least one prime factor congruent to 2 modulo 3,
as a function of the number of prime factors of n and of (n+3)/4. The object must
be a density statement with an error term, and its inputs must be the
factorisation shapes of the two factors rather than their sizes.
