An object that assigns to an odd integer congruent to 1 modulo 24 the least
modulus congruent to 3 modulo 4 at which the product of the integer with a fourth
of its shift by that modulus carries a divisor congruent to -1, and that expresses
a bound on that assignment in terms of the integer alone. The object must express
what forces the assignment upward, must express the growth rate the bound would
need, and must be stated so that a proof of the bound settles every value below
it by a finite check.
