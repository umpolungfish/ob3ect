An object that assigns to an ordered pair of finite graphs the least number of
vertices forcing, in every two-colouring of the edges of the complete graph on
that many vertices, a copy of the first graph in the first colour or a copy of
the second in the second. The object must make the assignment total, must express
monotonicity in each argument, and must expose the value as computable from the
colourings rather than as an infimum over a set that may be empty.
