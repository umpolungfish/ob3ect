An object over r-uniform hypergraphs that assigns to each pair (r, n) the least
number of vertices forcing a monochromatic complete r-uniform subhypergraph on n
vertices under two colourings, together with the construction that lifts a lower
bound at r to a lower bound at r+1. The object must carry both the number and the
lifting map, and must express the tower growth as a property of the lift.
