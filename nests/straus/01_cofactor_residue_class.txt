An object that, given an odd integer congruent to 1 modulo 24 and a positive
integer r congruent to 3 modulo 4, decides from the residues of n alone whether
the product n(n+r)/4 admits a divisor congruent to -1 modulo r, and exhibits that
divisor when it exists. The object is to be a decision structure over residues,
not a search over divisors: its input is the residue data of n at the primes
dividing r and at small primes, and its output is a divisor together with the
congruence it satisfies.
