An object that holds four simultaneous multiplicative conditions on a single
integer n: that n is a product of primes congruent to 1 modulo 4, that n+4 is,
that (n+1)/2 is, and that n(n+3)/4 is a product of primes congruent to 1 modulo
3. The object must express the joint density of integers satisfying all four, and
must express whether the conditions are independent or constrain one another.
