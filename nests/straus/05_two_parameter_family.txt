An object over pairs (k, w) of positive integers with w dividing kn+1, w coprime
to 4k, k dividing w+1, and (w+1)/k congruent to 3 modulo 4, for a fixed odd n
congruent to 1 modulo 24. The object must express when the family of such pairs
is empty, must express the least k when it is not, and must relate the parameter
k to the factorisation of kn+1.
