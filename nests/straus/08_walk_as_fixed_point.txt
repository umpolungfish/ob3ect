An object that presents the sequence of moduli r = 3, 7, 11, 15, ... acting on a
fixed odd integer n, where each modulus determines a set of reachable residues
from the divisors of n(n+r)/4, and the action terminates when zero is reachable.
The object must classify this action as one that arrives from a neighbourhood or
one that arrives only from the target itself, and must express the observable
that distinguishes the two after two steps.
