An object that assigns to each odd integer n congruent to 1 modulo 24 the least
positive integer r congruent to 3 modulo 4 such that n(n+r)/4 admits a divisor
congruent to -1 modulo r, and expresses that assignment as a function of the
factorisation data of n. The object is to make the assignment structural: its
value must be derived from arithmetic invariants of n, and it must say what
increases the value and what decreases it.
